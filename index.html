Open Access Resources

Scholarly tools and digital materials for the study of the early and central Middle Ages.

Recognizing the evolving needs of medievalists, members of the Haskins Society have curated this collection of Open Access Resources (OARs) to support research, pedagogy, and digital exploration of the Anglo-Saxon, Viking, and Norman worlds.
