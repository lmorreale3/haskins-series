Special Series

The Haskins Society OAR Series

Open Access Resources hosted on Middle Ages for Educators

This dedicated series features pedagogical tools and digital scholarship curated by members of the Haskins Society. Integrated into the Middle Ages for Educators platform, these resources provide specialized pathways into the history of the early and central Middle Ages.

Suggested Resource Models

We invite society members to contribute digital materials. Below are examples of the types of Open Access Resources (OARs) we aim to produce and host.

Model: Primary Source

Document Translation Sets

Suggested: Curated sets of Anglo-Norman or Viking-age primary sources with accompanying paleographical notes for classroom use.

Model: Digital Method

HTR & Manuscript Tutorials

Suggested: Step-by-step guides on using Handwritten Text Recognition for 11th-century charters and administrative records.

Model: Video

"Object in Focus" Shorts

Suggested: 5-minute video lectures from members highlighting a single material artifact or archaeological find.

Model: Pedagogy

Thematic Syllabi Modules

Suggested: Deep-dive modules into the "French of England" or "Viking Diaspora" intended for undergraduate survey courses.
